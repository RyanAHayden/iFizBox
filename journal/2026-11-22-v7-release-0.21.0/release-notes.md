A real iOS 26 tab bar, era icon sets, and era app icons with dark modes
iOS 26 style on iOS 26+: the tab bar is iOS's own, with its press-drag lens and shrink on scroll
iOS 13 and iOS 26 styles get their own icon sets for rows, tabs and Home
Theme > App Icon has iOS 7, iOS 13 and iOS 26 icons that turn dark with the Home Screen

- iOS 26 style on iOS 26+: the tab bar is a real iOS tab bar, with Search as its own circle, badges, the press-drag lens and the bar shrinking as you scroll, all drawn by iOS. The nav bar's buttons stay iOS's own Liquid Glass.
- iOS 13 and iOS 26 styles have their own icon sets: section rows, glyphs, guides, Home and filled tab bar icons. Row icons follow a Theme > Style change right away, so iOS 7 no longer shows iOS 6 icons after a switch.
- Theme > App Icon: a new Eras group (iOS 7, iOS 13, iOS 26). Each has a light and a dark icon, so the Home Screen icon turns dark by itself in Dark mode. Match Theme under the iOS 7, 13 and 26 styles picks that era's icon.
- The Running page draws in the iOS 26 style: segmented control, capsule progress bars, the stop glyph and capsule buttons, light and dark.
- Related tab: rows show the section's icon under the package's own picture instead of a letter tile.
- Lite: a Tweak Source's tweak page no longer says Can't Install Yet (needs mobilesubstrate).

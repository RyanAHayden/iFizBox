- **Glass icon**: a new Glass app icon under Special in Theme > App Icon. The Home Screen draws it with the system glass in Default, Dark, Clear and Tinted.
- **Package lists**: Installed and section lists use wide package rows like Cydia's. Install IPAs is now a plain row at the top of Installed's list.
- **This iPhone (Lite)**: a copy that loses its connection notices sooner and reconnects after about 8 seconds of quiet instead of 30. Its speed summary now counts only the time data was moving and shows waiting time on its own.
- **Home**: the green row is always More Sources.
- **Fixes**: author pages no longer crash; price buttons (SIGN, INSTALL, OPEN, ADD) use the iOS 7 and iOS 13 themes' look; iOS 13 shows no large title on Home.

**Since 0.17.1**, the 0.18 releases brought: iOS 7 style and an app icon that follows the theme or your own picture (0.18.0); re-signed apps keep their own keychain and app-group data (0.18.1); iFizBox keeps its signing certificate and sign-ins out of reach of other sideloaded apps (0.18.2); installs on This iPhone survive a dropped connection (0.18.3); re-sign from the IPA kept at install, and Add Tweaks lists tweaks for that app first (0.18.4).

Sideload (no jailbreak): iFizBox.ipa, or add https://ifizbox.com/repo/source.json in the app or AltStore.
Jailbroken (rootless): iFizBox.deb, or the built-in https://ifizbox.com/repo/ source.

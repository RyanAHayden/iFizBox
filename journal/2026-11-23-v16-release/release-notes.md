- The Install Switch now opens Shortcuts once per install queue on iOS 16 and later, and not again when you come back from an install unless it has to turn cellular data back on or bring your VPN back; the app asks for it only on cellular or when another VPN is on (to get it, delete Install Switch in the Shortcuts app, then tap Update on Settings > Installation & Signing > This iPhone > Install Switch).
- Swiping left or right on a package's or app's page moves between Details, Reviews and Related.
- Tapping a row in Install Apps With now picks that way, and holding an app's Install button queues its update straight away with an Undo bar instead of opening a menu.
- In a run of several apps, a signed app waiting its turn shows a full bar with moving stripes and says "Signed: Waiting to Install".
- The iOS 26 style keeps its top buttons steady when going back, opens menus from their own button (with icons in Share), no longer cuts Installed's Wish List button short, and is offered only on iOS 26 or later; on the website, iOS 6 is now the only style, with the others in the app.
- Smaller fixes: installed apps show under Needs Re-sign once, and signed apps keep a small log that helps find why an app lost its sign-in; the Lite app no longer recommends Chariz (searching for it still finds it, and a Chariz source already added stays); pages in Installation & Signing slide in and back; the website's computer and Android page has GitHub and Discord buttons; an AltStore source's app icon sits in its row; the page no longer scrolls while you swipe back; and in the iOS 6 style the black strip behind the clock and battery stays put while sheets, including the install log, slide up and down.

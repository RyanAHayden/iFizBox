Sideload app: a built-in VPN for This iPhone installs
Re-signed apps keep their logins: re-sign them once
Swipe back from anywhere on a page
What's New on top of Details when an update is out

- The sideload app installs on This iPhone through its own VPN, so Tailscale or LocalDevVPN is no longer needed: tap Set Up, then Test Connection, on the This iPhone page.
- Re-signed apps keep their logins and no longer crash on saved passwords: re-sign your apps once from Needs Re-sign.
- Swipe back from anywhere on a page, and an app's or package's Details shows What's New first when an update is out.
- The jailbreak app lists the phone's installed apps and can decrypt one into My IPAs.
- Tapping Install opens Confirm straight away (hold it for older versions), and pasting an address in Add Source adds it at once.
- Smaller fixes: iOS 26 menus pop out of their button, iOS 13 and iOS 26 menus and bars match their style, sheets no longer flash a black strip, and apt no longer breaks on a repo with a bad file.
